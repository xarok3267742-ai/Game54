# Asset Prompts

Play Store icon and feature graphic candidates are now generated from ImageGen sources and documented in `docs/play_store_icon.md` and `docs/feature_graphic_candidate.md`. The remaining prompts below are retained for future design iteration and non-release exploratory assets.

## App Icon Concept

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: Android adaptive app icon and Google Play icon. Primary request: a readable, friendly, non-scary icon for a Russian micro-cleaning productivity app named “Порядок 5”. Subject: five tidy rounded checklist tiles forming a compact stack, one clean completion check, one small home/order cue and a warm amber sparkle. Style: polished app-store raster, soft flat-vector/raster hybrid, calm sage/blue/amber palette, generous padding, high small-size readability. Constraints: no text, no letters, no numerals, no plant/pot, no tiny decorative scene, no mascots, no fake UI, no heavy dark circular marks.

## Feature Graphic Concept

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: Google Play feature graphic, 1024x500. Primary request: clean 3D product-style composition matching the current icon system: large checklist/notepad, compact timer ring, sage check badge and layered tidy sheets on warm off-white. Copy: avoid embedded text. Constraints: no small text, no fake screenshots, no plants, no pots, no cloth, no clutter, no random decorative objects.

## Onboarding Illustration Concept

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: onboarding illustration. Primary request: calm flat illustration of a tidy shelf, keys, folded cloth, and small checklist card. Style: minimal, warm, consistent with sage/blue/amber palette. Constraints: no characters, no brands, no text in image, no crowded room.

## Empty State Concept

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: empty state illustration. Primary request: simple open box with three neatly arranged household items and a small check mark. Style: flat, restrained, accessible contrast. Constraints: no text, no mascot, no complex scene.
